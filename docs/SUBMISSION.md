# Submission text

**Tagline:** Paste a confusing Malaysian broadband bill, understand it, and send a clear billing enquiry.

## Description (copy-paste)

BillWise is for ordinary Malaysian broadband customers who don't know billing terms and find their bill higher than expected. Paste a bill (or try the Unifi promo-expiry sample) and it explains each charge, ranks findings as act on this, worth checking, good to know or looks fine, and shows savings estimates with their assumptions. From a questionable charge you start a billing enquiry: provider, charge and amount carry over, and you get an editable draft in English or Bahasa Malaysia with an evidence checklist. Nothing is sent. You then track the case with the provider's reference, status and notes, linked to your browser, no sign-up. Rules are deterministic, with no AI. Sample bills are invented, and plan data comes from public provider pages checked Nov 2026. Independent, not affiliated with any provider. Live: https://billwise-blush.vercel.app

## Judge journey

1. Open the homepage, then "Try a sample bill" (Unifi promo-expiry sample), or paste your own bill.
2. Read the breakdown: each charge explained, findings ranked "Act on this", "Worth checking", "Good to know" or "Looks fine", savings estimates with their assumptions.
3. On a questionable charge, click to start a billing enquiry. Provider, charge, amount and analysis link carry over.
4. Create the case, get an editable enquiry in English or BM, copy it, see the evidence checklist. Nothing is sent to the provider.
5. Record the provider's reference, change status, add notes. Refresh and reopen: it is saved.
