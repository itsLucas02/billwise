- **Deadline:** Monday, 16 November 2026, 11:59 PM MYT
- **Reward:** 250 AI Credits

## About

Build a useful tool for the people who use, support, or improve connectivity.

### The challenge

Connectivity problems don’t end at “no signal.” A customer may struggle to understand a bill or choose a plan. A support agent may have to piece together the same complaint from scattered reports. A field team may arrive without the information it needs.

Choose one person, one telecom-related problem, and one useful outcome. Then build a working tool that gets them there.

Use any AI coding tools or stack you like. We’re looking for a finished, usable workflow—not the biggest feature list.

### Need a starting point?

- **Bill Detective** — Make charges easier to understand and help customers prepare questions for support.
- **Dead-Zone Diary** — Record poor-connection experiences and turn them into a clear report. User reports are not verified coverage data.
- **Connectivity Troubleshooter** — Help someone work through practical checks and capture what they tried.
- **Outage Comms Studio** — Help a team turn a fictional outage timeline into clear, consistent customer updates.
- **Support Pattern Finder** — Find recurring issues in synthetic support tickets and show the evidence behind them.
- **Plan Fit & Gap Finder** — Match a customer’s needs to fictional or clearly sourced, dated plans—and show where the options fall short.
- **Field Visit Prep** — Turn a mock fault report into a useful technician brief and post-visit handoff.

These are ideas, not assigned topics. If you see a better problem worth solving, build that.

### What counts as a submission

Build a working tool, not just a concept or a set of screens. Someone should be able to use it to complete one meaningful task and come away with a result or next step.

When you submit, tell us who it is for, what problem it solves, and what you built.

Use synthetic or properly anonymised data. Label estimates, mock scenarios, and user-submitted reports clearly. Don’t use private customer records, proprietary network access, or a telecom provider’s branding without permission.

### What we’ll look for

Does it solve a real problem? Does the workflow work from start to finish? Is the result useful and easy to understand? Has the builder handled data and uncertainty responsibly? Does the execution show thought and craft?

Build it. Submit it. Showcase it.

## Requirements

1. Build a working telecom-related tool for a clearly defined user: a customer, support agent, network team, or field technician.
2. Solve one specific problem through a complete flow, from the user’s input to a useful result or next step. Static mockups alone do not qualify.
3. Submit a link to the working tool so it can be reviewed and showcased. No presentation or demo video is required.
4. Include a short description of who the tool is for, the problem it solves, and how to use it.
5. Use sample, synthetic, or properly anonymised data; clearly label simulated scenarios, estimates, and user-submitted reports.
6. Do not use private customer data, proprietary telecom systems, or a provider’s name, logo, or branding without permission.

> "Build it. Submit it. Showcase it." describes the competition process. BillWise is the tool being submitted; it must not contain a bounty portal, submission wall or voting feature.
