## Skipped provider and unavailable variants

- **TIME (TIME dotCom):** The consumer site (`https://www.time.com.my/`), corporate site (`https://corporate.time.com.my/`) and business site returned HTTP 403; Chrome displayed a Cloudflare block on the consumer site. The official sign-up page (`https://ols.time.com.my/order`) did expose a black inline TIME wordmark, but a monochrome version does not meet the requested full-colour primary-logo requirement. No `time.svg` or `time.png` was saved. No unofficial substitute was used.
- **Unifi:** No separate icon-only SVG/PNG located on the inspected homepage. Its ICO favicon was not converted.
- **Allo:** No distinct symbol-only version located in the inspected homepage and official media-library logo results. The complete lockup was retained.
- **Yes:** The primary mark already includes the Yes name in its speech symbol. No different icon-only SVG/PNG was identified; duplicate/lower-resolution copies were omitted.
- Maxis and U Mobile icon PNGs are officially linked favicons, rather than brand-kit icon downloads. Their native sizes are recorded above; they were not enlarged or converted.

## Usage guidance and restrictions encountered

These notes record what the sites say; downloading is not a grant of permission to use the marks in BillWise.

- **TM / Unifi:** The [TM Website Use & Access Agreement](https://tm.com.my/sites/default/files/Documents/TM_Website_Use_2013_0.pdf), linked from Unifi's homepage, is dated 19 September 2013. Section 9 says commercial use requires express prior written consent and that logos/graphics/images may not be copied or retransmitted unless expressly permitted by TM. This was the linked document observed on the retrieval date.
- **Maxis:** [Terms of Use — Copyright and Trademark Notice](https://www.maxis.com.my/en/terms-of-use/) limits use to personal/non-commercial purposes and requires prior written consent for reuse/display/storage and use of its logos/trademarks; access grants no implied logo licence.
- **TIME:** [Website Usage](https://www.time.com.my/terms-and-conditions/website-usage), sections 5 and 15, limits content use and reserves trademark rights; uses outside its stated permissions require written permission. The terms page was readable through web retrieval even though local logo retrieval was blocked. No TIME image is included.
- **U Mobile:** [Media Depository](https://www.u.com.my/en/about-us/newsroom/media-depository) explicitly offers its logo for download, distinguishing RGB for digital use and Pantone for print. No additional logo-specific clearance/spacing/minimum-size rules were displayed in the inspected logo-download section.
- **CelcomDigi:** The inspected [Terms & Conditions page](https://www.celcomdigi.com/terms-and-conditions) carries an all-rights-reserved copyright notice. No logo-specific usage licence or design guidelines were identified in the pages inspected.
- **Allo and Yes:** No affirmative third-party logo-use licence or logo-specific usage guidelines were identified in the inspected pages/media metadata. Yes's Terms of Use route led to `https://enterprisecrm.yes.my/docs/tnc/general-terms-and-conditions/terms-of-use-of-website/`, whose retrieved page did not expose readable terms to the tools used; its terms were not verified.
